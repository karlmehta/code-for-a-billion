# Judging Rubric — Code for a Billion

Every submission is scored out of **100 points** across five criteria. Judges review your
**problem statement**, **GitHub repo**, and **demo** from your submission issue.

| # | Criterion | Weight | What judges look for |
|---|-----------|:------:|----------------------|
| 1 | **Impact & Problem Fit** | 30 | Does it solve a real, meaningful problem? How many people could it help, and how much? Clarity of the problem statement and who it's for. |
| 2 | **Working Solution** | 25 | Does the demo actually work end-to-end? Completeness vs. a mockup. Quality and reliability of what was built. |
| 3 | **Technical Execution** | 20 | Code quality, architecture, and sensible use of **AgentFoundry (AF)**. Repo is clean, documented, and reproducible. |
| 4 | **Innovation & Creativity** | 15 | Originality of the approach. Non-obvious insight or a clever use of AI/agents. |
| 5 | **Demo & Communication** | 10 | Is the demo clear and compelling? Can a judge understand the value in under 3 minutes? |

**Total: 100**

## How scoring works

- Each judge scores independently, then scores are averaged per submission.
- Ties are broken by **Impact & Problem Fit** (criterion 1), then **Working Solution** (criterion 2).
- Judges may leave written feedback as a comment on your submission issue.

## To score well

- **Lead with the problem.** Make the "who" and "how big" obvious in the first two sentences.
- **Ship something that runs.** A small working slice beats a big non-working vision.
- **Make the repo reproducible.** A README with setup steps judges can follow.
- **Keep the demo tight.** 2–3 minutes, show the real thing, no slideware.
- **Show your AgentFoundry usage.** Note where and how AF helped you build.

## Eligibility

- Built using **AgentFoundry (AF)**, the official IDE.
- Public GitHub repository with a working README.
- A demo link (live app or video) that judges can access.
- Submission issue filed before the deadline.

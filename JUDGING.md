# Judging Rubric — Code for a Billion

Every submission is scored out of **100 points** across four criteria, **weighted 25% each**.
Judges review your **problem statement**, **GitHub repo**, **demo**, and **deployment/impact data**
from your submission issue.

| # | Criterion | Weight | What judges look for |
|---|-----------|:------:|----------------------|
| 1 | **Size of the problem** | 25 | How many citizens face this problem? The larger the number of people affected, the higher the score. |
| 2 | **Severity of the problem** | 25 | The level of human suffering, or the economic loss, caused by the problem. |
| 3 | **Quality of the solution** | 25 | How well the solution is designed and how effective it is at actually solving the problem. |
| 4 | **Proven impact** | 25 | Actual deployment data — real evidence of impact in the field (users reached, outcomes, measurable change). |

**Total: 100**

## How scoring works

- Each judge scores independently, then scores are averaged per submission.
- Ties are broken by **Proven impact** (criterion 4) — real deployment data wins — then **Size of the problem** (criterion 1).
- Judges may leave written feedback as a comment on your submission issue.

## To score well

- **Quantify the reach.** State how many citizens face the problem, with a credible source or estimate.
- **Show the stakes.** Make the human suffering or economic loss concrete — numbers, not adjectives.
- **Prove effectiveness.** Don't just build it — show it works and measurably moves the problem.
- **Deploy and measure.** The strongest submissions have real usage in the field with data to back it: people reached, outcomes changed, before/after. Include this in your submission.

## Eligibility

- Built using **AgentFoundry (AF)**, the official IDE.
- Public GitHub repository with a working README.
- A demo link (live app or video) that judges can access.
- Submission issue filed before the deadline.
